A Heart for Our Youth

Pastor Jonathan has a deep passion for raising up the next generation. We're in the planning stages of a youth ministry where teens will grow through Bible study, real conversations, and genuine fellowship — equipped and empowered to own their faith.

The timing matters: nationally, Gen Z is now attending church nearly twice as often as they were in 2020 — the highest rate Barna Group has recorded since the generation first came of age (State of the Church, 2025). We want Christ Church Bluffton ready to welcome them.
