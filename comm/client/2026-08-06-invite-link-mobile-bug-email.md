Hi all,

Quick heads up before you click your invite link.

I found a bug today: if the invite link is opened on a phone, the phone's mail app can sometimes "preview" the link in the background before you actually tap it — and that quietly uses up the link, since it only works once. When that happens, tapping it yourself does nothing, and there's no obvious error, it just doesn't work.

Two people ran into exactly this today. I've since fixed the bug for any new invites going forward, but to be safe:

**Please open your invite link on a computer, not your phone, when you get to it.**

If you've already tried clicking it on your phone (even if it seemed to do nothing), just let me know and I'll send you a fresh link right away.

Sorry for the hassle — appreciate your patience while this is still new.
