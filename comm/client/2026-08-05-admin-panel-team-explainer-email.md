Subject: New: Church Admin Panel — What It Is and How to Use It

Hi all,

First off SORRY for the long email but I wanted to write about what I've been working on.

I've built a private admin panel for the church website. It's a simple online tool where you can manage prayer requests, congregant records, upcoming events, volunteers, and photos — all in one place, without needing to touch any code or ask me for changes every time. This is still a work in progress, so things may change. If there's anything you need that's missing, or something you don't need at all, just let me know.

You can get to it here: christchurchbluffton.org/admin

What it does:
- Keeps a running list of everyone who's signed up for the newsletter or reached out through the contact form
- Collects prayer requests people submit on the website — including ones people leave anonymously — so nothing gets missed
- Keeps a record of congregant families — who's part of the church, their contact info, kids, that kind of thing
- Lets you list upcoming events and keep track of volunteer sign-ups (plans to tie to website in the future)
- Lets you upload photos and get a direct link to share
- Keeps track of church staff
- Allows text/image edits for the website without a developer (Not live but viewable)
- Create newsletters through click & drag, code, or AI prompts (Not live but viewable)
- Account permissions
- Review logs for Site Admins
- Controls account permissions, so each person only has access to what fits their role.

Other ideas I'm considering:
Nothing here is built yet, just floating these to see what's actually useful:
- Budget or spreadsheet access
- Some kind of internal chat, or AI tools built into the panel
- A built-in payment system

** Ideally, as the church grows, I'd like to help keep costs down. There will likely be a need for third-party systems that come with monthly fees, but building things ourselves where possible helps reduce those costs. If you have any ideas let me know!

Who has access:
Each of you has your own private login. Depending on your role, you'll see either everything or the specific parts that make sense for what you do.

Getting started:
Keep an eye out for a separate email from me with your invite—it'll have a link in it. Click the link and it'll take you straight in, no separate login step first — you'll just be asked to create your own password right there, and then you're in.

I've done my own testing on everything, but if you spot anything that looks off, please don't hesitate to let me know so I can get it fixed.

Right now, Newsletter and the Content Editor (the website editor) aren't live yet—but feel free to click into them and see how they work while you're logged in. You'll notice green and red notes throughout the panel letting you know what's actually live versus what's still being tested. Right now, all prayer requests, newsletter signups, and contact form fill-outs on the website automatically populate in the panel. It's still tied to Tithe.ly (Breeze), so some of you will keep getting email notifications for these the same way you always have.

At this time, you don't need to worry about adding newsletter subscribers. I can bulk-add those to the server on my end to save time, and I don't think we'll need to touch that until the newsletter feature is actually up and running. Otherwise, feel free to poke around—add a staff member, a congregant family, a photo, an event, whatever you'd like to try out.

If anything's confusing or doesn't work the way you'd expect, just let me know and I'll take a look.

Kevin
