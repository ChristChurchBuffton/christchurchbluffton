Here's a quick rundown of how to add photos in the admin panel and get a link you can share.

Adding a photo:
1. Go to the Photos page in the sidebar.
2. Click "Upload."
3. Type a name for the photo (like "Church Crew" or "Fall Picnic") — it'll turn that into a clean file name automatically, so you don't have to worry about formatting it yourself.
4. Choose the photo from your computer and confirm. Photos need to be JPG files — if yours is a different format, let me know and I can help convert it.

Getting a link to share:
Every photo — whether it was already there or one you just added — has a "Copy Link" button on it. Click that and it copies a real, ready-to-use link you can paste into a text, email, or wherever you need it. Clicking directly on a photo also opens that same link.

That's really it—it takes about 20 seconds per photo once you've done it a couple times. Let me know if anything trips you up.
