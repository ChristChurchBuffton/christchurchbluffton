## Bio

The Rev. Jonathan H. Riddle is an Anglican priest and church planter serving in Bluffton, South Carolina. After more than a decade of ministry leadership at The Church of the Cross, he is now planting Christ Church Bluffton with a vision to cultivate a faithful, Gospel-centered presence in the Lowcountry. Jonathan is passionate about forming communities shaped by Scripture, shared meals, and lives arranged for the glory of God and the good of others.

He is a graduate of Appalachian State University and holds a Master of Divinity from Reformed Theological Seminary. Jonathan and his wife, Lisa, have been married for 29 years and are grateful parents to six incredible children. He can happily talk for hours about C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien, and the Carolina Panthers.

## Mission Statement (may be modified later)

To plant a reproducing Anglican church in Bluffton and beyond. A faith community shaped by Scripture, Prayer, and Sacramental Life, joining a Gospel movement that fosters personal conversion and authentic community where every person is known and valued. That our faithful Christian presence makes Bluffton a better and more hopeful place to live.
